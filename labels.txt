Income
income.employment:	Salary and wages from an employer.
income.public:	Benefits and allowances from public bodies, such as child benefit from Försäkringskassan.
income.sales:	Selling your own things on a marketplace such as Tradera or Blocket.
income.capital:	Interest and dividends.

Spending

spend.groceries:	Supermarkets and food shops.
spend.dining:	Restaurants, cafés, fast food and food delivery.
spend.transport:	Public transport, taxis and ride-hailing, scooters, fuel, parking, bridge tolls.
spend.shopping:	Clothes, beauty, home and furniture, electronics, DIY, online stores.
spend.leisure:	Streaming, games, gym, cinema, events, flights, hotels, theme parks.
spend.health:	Pharmacies, doctors and dentists.
spend.family:	Childcare and school fees.
spend.gambling:	Betting, casinos and lotteries, deposits and winnings alike.

Credit
credit.loan:	Repaying a loan, a credit card bill or a pay-later invoice.
credit.collections:	Paying a debt collector or Kronofogden, the enforcement authority.

Transfers
transfer.own:	Between the customer's own everyday accounts, credit card account included.
transfer.savings:	Into or out of the customer's own savings.
transfer.person:	To or from a private person, with no purpose stated.
transfer.cash:	Cash withdrawals.
